Bodyweight Squat

Home Workout Planner Recommended Builds Workout Timer

Muscle Group: Back Target Muscles: Upper Back Equipment: Bodyweight

Instructions

Step 1: Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, with your hands either extended straight out in front of you, crossed over your chest, or placed behind your head.

Step 2: Lower your body into a squat position, keeping your back straight, driving your hips back as if sitting in a chair, and keeping your knees in line with your feet.

Step 3: Push through your heels to stand back up, straightening your legs and squeezing your glutes at the top.

Step 4: Pause for a moment at the top, then slowly lower your body back down to repeat the movement.

Step 5: Repeat for the desired number of repetitions.
