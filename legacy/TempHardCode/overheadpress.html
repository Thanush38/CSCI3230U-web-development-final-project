Overhead Press

Home Workout Planner Recommended Builds Workout Timer

Muscle Group: Shoulders Target Muscles: Delts Equipment: Dumbbell

Instructions

Step 1: Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder level with your palms facing forward.

Step 2: Press the dumbbells upward until your arms are fully extended overhead.

Step 3: Pause for a moment at the top, then slowly lower the dumbbells back down to shoulder level.

Step 4: Repeat for the desired number of repetitions.
